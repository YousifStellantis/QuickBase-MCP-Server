# Modern automotive plant skills catalog

A starting catalog to propose competencies for positions. It reflects common practice
and published industry skill gaps (PLC/robot maintenance, high-voltage battery work,
mechatronics); it is not an official Stellantis standard. Align names and levels with
the plant training department.

## EV high voltage and battery
- High-voltage safety awareness (all staff in HV areas)
- HV authorization for work on de-energized systems (lockout, verification of absence of voltage, PPE)
- Battery pack and module handling (lifting aids, damage inspection, thermal event response)
- Battery assembly processes: module stacking, busbar torque, insulation resistance and
  hi-pot testing, leak testing, end-of-line battery tests
- Thermal incident response and quarantine procedures

## Robotics and automation
- Robot operation and recovery (KUKA, FANUC, ABB): teach pendant, jogging, safe zones
- Robot programming: KUKA KRL, FANUC TP, path and tool-center-point corrections
- Robot maintenance: mastering/calibration, dress packs, grippers and end effectors
- Collaborative robots: risk assessment basics, force/speed limits
- AGVs/AMRs and conveyors (EMS, skillets, power-and-free): faults, recovery, PM

## Controls and electrical
- PLC troubleshooting: Rockwell Studio 5000 / ControlLogix, Siemens TIA Portal / S7-1500
- HMI/SCADA navigation and alarm handling
- Industrial networks: EtherNet/IP, PROFINET, IO-Link; device replacement
- Safety circuits and safety PLCs: light curtains, interlocks, safety relays (machinery
  functional safety concepts per ISO 13849 / IEC 62061)
- Electrical troubleshooting: schematics, motor drives/VFDs, 24 V and 480/600 V systems
- Electrical safety work practices (arc flash, LOTO), e.g. NFPA 70E / CSA Z462 in North America

## Assembly technologies
- DC electric torque tools and controllers (e.g. Atlas Copco, Desoutter): strategies
  (torque, torque-angle), error-proofing, tool changeovers, traceability of results
- Error-proofing (poka-yoke) devices and verification
- Glazing / urethane bead dispensing and inspection (glass cell)
- Adhesives, sealers and fastening (body tub cell, closures)
- Wiring harness routing and connector seating checks
- End-of-line: wheel alignment, ADAS calibration, roll/brake test, water test

## Inspection and quality
- Automated / AI visual inspection (AVI) cells: lighting, camera alignment, model
  re-training requests, false-call handling
- Metrology: gauges, CMM basics, gap/flush measurement
- Problem solving: 5-Why, Ishikawa, 8D participation, QRQC
- SPC reading and reaction plans; MSA participation

## Maintenance technologies
- Autonomous maintenance steps (clean, inspect, lubricate, tighten), standard work
- Planned/professional maintenance: PM routes, CMMS work orders, spare-parts usage
- Condition monitoring: vibration analysis, thermography, ultrasound, oil analysis
- Pneumatics and hydraulics troubleshooting

## Digital and data
- MES and traceability screens (build records, genealogy)
- Quickbase/low-code literacy: reading reports, entering data correctly, simple dashboards
- Data literacy: reading control charts, Pareto, run charts; asking for the right report
- Responsible use of AI assistants and copilots (data classification, no confidential
  data in public tools, verifying outputs)
- OT cybersecurity awareness (USB, remote access, phishing on the floor)

## Lean / continuous improvement
- Standard work and job instruction (TWI-style)
- SMED changeover, kaizen events, A3 thinking, PDCA
- Loss analysis and cost deployment basics
- 5S / workplace organization audits

## People and leadership (team leader / supervisor)
- Shift start-up meetings and tier boards (SQDCP)
- Coaching and on-the-job training delivery (to level 4)
- Safety leadership: observations, near-miss reporting, risk assessment
